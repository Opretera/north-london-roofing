Roof repair costs depend on the roof type, access, materials, the size of the damaged area and whether the work is urgent. A small tile or flashing repair may cost a few hundred pounds, while widespread damage or structural work can cost considerably more. The safest approach is to request a written quotation showing the labour, materials and agreed scope.

Warning signs include pressure to pay the full amount upfront, no written quotation, unclear business details, unwillingness to explain the work, changing prices without agreement and claims that every part of the roof needs replacing without evidence. Choose a roofer who provides clear contact information, insurance details, a written scope and realistic explanations.

Common repairs include replacing broken or slipped tiles, sealing failed flashing, fixing leaks around chimneys and valleys, repairing damaged flat-roof membranes and correcting blocked or leaking gutters. Water entry can travel, so the visible damp area is not always directly beneath the original defect.

Hourly labour rates vary according to location, experience, access, roof type and urgency. Standard work may be priced per roofer or as a fixed quotation, while emergency and out-of-hours work normally costs more. A written fixed-price quotation is usually easier to compare than an hourly figure on its own.
