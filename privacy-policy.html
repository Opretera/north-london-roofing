Privacy Policy

How North London Roofing Ltd handles information and website use.

Information We Collect

When you submit an enquiry, we may collect your name, email address, postcode, property details and any information you include in your message.

How We Use Your Information

We use enquiry information to respond to you, assess roofing requirements, prepare quotations and communicate about requested services.

How Enquiries Are Processed

The website enquiry form is processed through FormSubmit and delivered to bootalr4@gmail.com. Do not submit highly sensitive personal or financial information through the form.

Sharing Information

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared only where needed to deliver a requested service, comply with the law or protect legitimate business interests.

Data Retention

Enquiry records may be kept for as long as reasonably needed for communication, quotations, record keeping and legal obligations.

Your Rights

You may ask what personal information is held about you, request corrections or ask for deletion where applicable. Contact bootalr4@gmail.com.
