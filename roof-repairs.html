Professional roofing service

Roof repairs that solve the source of the problem.

Professional roof repairs in North London for leaking, damaged and weathered roofs, with careful diagnosis and targeted repair work.

Clear advice before any work begins.

Roof problems rarely improve by themselves. A small defect around a tile, flashing detail or roof junction can allow water to travel before it becomes visible indoors. Our repair approach focuses on tracing the likely cause, explaining the options and completing proportionate work.

What this service can include

  • Leaking roof investigation
  • Broken or slipped tile replacement
  • Lead flashing and roof-junction repairs
  • Storm and wind damage repairs
  • Chimney and ridge detail repairs
  • Preventative maintenance

Why the inspection matters

A good repair should address the defect rather than simply cover the visible symptom. We assess the surrounding roof area, consider how water is moving and explain whether a local repair or wider renewal is the sensible option.

What you receive

Where appropriate, you will receive a written quotation describing the proposed work, materials and expected timescale. Any assumptions or access requirements should be stated clearly before the project starts.
