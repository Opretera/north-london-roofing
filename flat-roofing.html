Modern flat roofing with clean, durable detailing.

Flat roofing in North London for extensions, garages, dormers and commercial spaces, installed with close attention to edges, outlets and drainage.

Clear advice before any work begins.

Flat roofs depend on correct preparation, falls and edge detailing. Many failures occur around outlets, upstands and penetrations rather than across the open roof area. Our assessment considers the full build-up and drainage route.

What this service can include

  • Flat-roof leak investigation
  • Replacement roof systems
  • Warm-roof insulation upgrades
  • Outlet and drainage improvements
  • Upstand and edge detailing
  • Garage and extension roofs

Why the inspection matters

The most suitable system depends on access, roof use, existing construction and budget. We explain the options without forcing every building into the same specification.

What you receive

Where appropriate, you will receive a written quotation describing the proposed work, materials and expected timescale. Any assumptions or access requirements should be stated clearly before the project starts.
