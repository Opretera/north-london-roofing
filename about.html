Our approach

Over 10 Years of Roofing Experience Across London

North London Roofing Ltd has over 10 years of practical roofing experience and a team of more than 50 roofing professionals. Across that time, the company has worked at almost 10,000 homes and apartments throughout London, covering repairs, replacements, flat roofs, gutters and urgent roofing problems.

The intended positioning is simple: inspect carefully, explain honestly, carry out the agreed work properly and leave the property tidy.

  • Professional communication from first enquiry
  • Clear project scopes and realistic expectations
  • Respect for occupied homes and working buildings
